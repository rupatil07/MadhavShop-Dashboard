# 🛍️ Madhav Shop Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Madhav Shop Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and profitability across different regions and categories. The dashboard transforms raw business data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions and improve overall business performance.

This dashboard helps business owners, sales managers, and analysts monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify growth opportunities, and track sales trends efficiently.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Primary data visualization and reporting platform.
* 📂 **Power Query** – Data extraction, cleaning, and transformation.
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Custom measures, KPIs, and calculated columns.
* 🔗 **Data Modeling** – Relationships between sales, customer, product, and regional datasets.
* 📁 **File Format**

  * `.pbit` – Power BI Template
  * `.pbix` – Development File
  * `.png` – Dashboard Screenshots

---

## 📂 Data Source

The dashboard uses retail sales transaction data containing:

* Order Details
* Customer Information
* Product Categories
* Sales Revenue
* Profit/Loss Metrics
* Regional Performance
* Payment Methods
* Order Quantities

The dataset is structured to support detailed analysis of business performance across multiple dimensions.

## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 📈 Business Problem

Retail businesses often struggle to answer critical questions such as:

* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which categories contribute the most profit?
* What regions perform best in terms of sales?
* Which customers drive the most business?
* How do sales trends change over time?

Without a centralized analytical solution, identifying these insights becomes time-consuming and inefficient.

---

### 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The primary objectives of this dashboard are to:

* Monitor overall sales performance.
* Track profitability across categories and regions.
* Identify top-performing products and customers.
* Analyze sales trends over time.
* Support strategic business decision-making.

---

### 📊 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

#### KPI Cards

Provides a high-level overview of:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value

#### Sales Trend Analysis

* Line charts showing monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Helps identify seasonal patterns and growth opportunities.

#### Category Performance

* Visual comparison of sales and profit by product category.
* Highlights the most profitable business segments.

#### Regional Analysis

* Sales distribution across states, cities, or regions.
* Identifies high-performing and underperforming markets.

#### Customer Insights

* Top customers based on revenue contribution.
* Customer purchasing behavior analysis.

#### Product Performance

* Best-selling products by quantity and revenue.
* Profitability analysis for individual products.

#### Payment Method Analysis

* Distribution of orders by payment mode.
* Helps understand customer payment preferences.

---

### 💡 Business Impact & Insights

#### Revenue Optimization

Identify high-performing products and categories to focus marketing and inventory investments.

#### Profitability Analysis

Track profit margins and eliminate low-performing products.

#### Customer Retention

Recognize valuable customers and develop targeted engagement strategies.

#### Regional Growth Planning

Discover markets with strong growth potential and allocate resources effectively.

#### Data-Driven Decision Making

Enable management to make informed strategic decisions based on real-time business insights.
